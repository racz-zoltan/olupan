User guide

Olupan is an offline workout log: log sets during your workout, body data, steps, food and stats – no account, all data on your device.

Install

iPhone, Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android, Chrome: menu → Install app. It then starts from its own icon, full screen, even offline. On iPhone the home-screen app and Safari use separate storage – stick to one.

Workouts

  • Start: from a template (Start) or empty. An empty workout is also saved as a template under My workouts when finished.
  • Sets: enter weight and reps (distance and time for cardio), then check the set. The “Previous” column shows your last session.
  • Rest time: starts when you check a set; tap the time under an exercise name to change it. The screen stays awake during workouts; if you put the app in the background, you get a notification when rest ends (if allowed).
  • Finish: only checked sets are saved. The app tells you about new records.

Body and steps

On the Body tab you can log weight, body fat, waist and steps daily. On iPhone, steps, weight and body fat can also be imported from the Health app with a Shortcuts shortcut – see the guide under the ? button.

Food

Fill in your profile (Settings → Profile and goals) to get a suggested daily energy and macro budget. Log food manually or with Claude: photograph or describe what you ate in your own Claude project, then paste the code block from the reply (Paste from Claude). Setup steps are in the Food tab’s help.

Calendar and stats

Calendar shows workouts, measurements and food by day. Stats has weight and body fat trends, energy balance, steps, workout count, volume, strength trends, personal records and a combined trend showing what moves together with your weight.

Backup, export, import

  • Backup: Settings → Save / share – put the file on iCloud, Google Drive or in Files. With no server, this is the only way to restore everything on a new device: back up regularly.
  • CSV export: workouts, body data, steps and food as spreadsheets.
  • Import: restore a backup file, or import workouts from CSV – the Sample button gives you an example file.

Settings

Language (Hungarian / English), start screen, light or dark appearance, accent colour, default rest time, sounds and haptics, notifications, keep screen awake, daily step goal.
