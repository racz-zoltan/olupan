1. Health and exercise

  • Before starting a new exercise programme or diet – especially with a chronic condition, injury, pregnancy, medication or after a long break – consult a doctor.
  • If you feel pain, dizziness, chest pressure or shortness of breath while exercising, stop immediately and seek medical help if needed.
  • You exercise at your own risk and should adapt workouts to your own abilities. Templates and exercises are general examples, not a personalised training plan.
  • If you have or have had an eating disorder, tracking calories and body weight can be harmful. Please seek professional support, and don’t use Olupan’s food and goal features.
  • The app is intended for adults (18+); suggested values apply to adults.

2. Estimates and calculations

Values shown by Olupan are informational estimates and may be inaccurate:

  • daily energy need and macro suggestions are based on the Mifflin–St Jeor equation and general guidelines;
  • estimated one-rep max (1RM) is an approximation from the Epley formula;
  • trends and correlations show statistical co-movement, not cause and effect;
  • nutrition values estimated with Claude (AI) – especially from photos – can be 20–30% off;
  • the accuracy of step, weight and body fat data depends on the measuring device (phone, watch, scale).
