Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 November 2026

Olupan is an offline workout log. Everything you enter – workouts, body data, food, your profile – is stored only on your own device. This policy explains, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, (EU) 2016/679), what happens to your data when you use Olupan (olupan.com, the “Service”).

1. Who is the controller?

Rácz Zoltán, a private individual, developer and operator of Olupan. Contact: [KAPCSOLATI E-MAIL].

2. Data you record in the app – we don’t have it

  • Only on your device: workouts, templates, body and step data, food, your profile (sex, birth year, height, activity, goal) and settings are kept in your browser’s local storage (localStorage) on your device. They are never sent to us or to any other server; there is no account, sign-up or sync.
  • Health data: weight, body fat, waist, steps and food are health-related data. Because neither the controller nor anyone else can access them, we do not process them; access to them depends on how your device is protected (passcode, biometrics).
  • Deletion: you can delete your data at any time: Settings → Delete everything, clearing the browser’s site data, or removing the home-screen app. As there is no server-side copy, deletion is permanent.
  • No tracking: we use no cookies, analytics, advertising IDs or third-party scripts. Fonts and icons are also served from our own host, not from an external provider.

3. What you start yourself – other providers

Some features use an external service of your choice. In that case data reaches them from your device at your decision, under their terms; Olupan itself sends them nothing.

  • Food logging with Claude: the “Claude” button opens your own Claude project in Anthropic’s service. What you describe or photograph there goes to Anthropic (Anthropic Privacy Policy). Only what you paste from the clipboard enters Olupan. The Meal suggestion buttons copy a request to the clipboard (your daily target, today’s meals and your food preferences); it only reaches Anthropic if you paste it into Claude, and you can review it before pasting. The training plan request works the same way (goal, training days, equipment and your exercise names).
  • Apple Health / Shortcuts: step, weight and body fat data are written to a file or the clipboard by a shortcut you create; Olupan reads it on the device. No data leaves your phone.
  • Backup and export: backup and CSV files are handed over through your phone’s share sheet – e.g. to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or the Files app. The terms of the storage provider you choose apply.
  • Notifications: the end-of-rest notification is shown by your device’s operating system; there is no push server, and you can revoke permission at any time in your phone’s settings.

4. Hosting provider (Vercel)

The Service’s files are delivered by Vercel Inc. (USA). When the page loads, Vercel – as a processor – may handle technical log data: IP address, time of request, requested file, browser and system type. We do not use these for identification, analysis or profiling.

  • Purpose and legal basis: secure and reliable delivery of the Service and prevention of abuse – legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f).
  • Retention: for a short period, according to Vercel’s log retention practice.
  • Transfers: for transfers outside the European Union (USA), Vercel applies safeguards recognised under EU law. Details: Vercel Privacy Policy.

After the first visit the app is stored on your device (service worker) and works offline; on updates only the program files are downloaded again.

5. Contacting us by email

If you email us, we process only what you provide in your message (email address, your name if given, and the content), solely to reply.

  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in responding to enquiries – GDPR Art. 6(1)(f).
  • Retention: until the enquiry is closed, at most 90 days; unsolicited or irrelevant messages may be deleted immediately. We don’t pass the data to third parties or use it for marketing.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to request information about data processed about you, its rectification, erasure or restriction, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Send your request to the email address above; we reply within one month. Data you record in the app is accessible only to you and managed directly by you.

You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in your country of residence. In Hungary: Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), Falk Miksa utca 9–11, 1055 Budapest; postal: 1363 Budapest, Pf. 9; email: ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu; naih.hu. You may also go to court.

7. Privacy by design and by default (GDPR Art. 25)

  • No data collection: the app’s logic runs entirely in your browser; there is no backend.
  • Protection by default: a strict Content Security Policy (CSP), integrity-checked program files (SRI), no external code and no tracking.
  • In your hands: only you can create and share backups; all imported data is validated.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its effective date. Before introducing any new processing, we will inform you separately.
